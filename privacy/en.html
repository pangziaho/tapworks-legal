Privacy Policy

TapWorks · Updated 4 October 2026

TapWorks reads NFC cards and tags entirely on your iPhone. Your cards, transactions and photos never leave your device unless you export them yourself. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What the app accesses

  • NFC. Used only after you tap Read Card or Write, to read or write the card or tag you hold to the iPhone. What is read is decoded on the device and stored in the app’s private storage.
  • Camera. Used only when you photograph a card or scan a barcode. Images are processed on the device.
  • Photos. Picking photos uses the system picker, which gives the app only the photos you select.
  • Contacts. Saving a business card from a tag uses the system’s new-contact screen, and choosing a contact to write uses the system picker, which gives the app only the contact you pick. The app never reads your contact list.
  • Wi-Fi. When you tap Join Network on a Wi-Fi tag, iOS asks you to confirm and then configures the network itself.
  • Place search. When you search for a place to write to a tag, the search text is sent to Apple Maps to find it. Nothing else is sent.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. If you lock the app or a card, the system checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded.
  • iCloud Drive (optional). If you turn on iCloud backup, a backup file is stored in your iCloud Drive by Apple. We have no access to it.
  • Widgets and system search (optional). Widgets show the last balances read, from data kept on the device. Only card names are added to the on-device search index; numbers and balances are not, and locked cards are left out.

What we do not do

  • No data is collected, transmitted to us or stored on our servers. The app has no servers.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, email or password is required.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Bundled data

City, card and station names for Chinese transit cards come from the Trip Reader Database (© domosekai and contributors, CC BY 4.0) and are included in the app. Looking them up happens on the device.

Purchases

TapWorks is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, Apple Account or billing details. There are no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

You can delete cards and scan history in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Backups in iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them.

Children

TapWorks is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
